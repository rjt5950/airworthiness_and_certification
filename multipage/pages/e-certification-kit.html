Technical Domain

The core technical section of the society: proposed explanations and reference structures covering airworthiness fundamentals, certification practice, and emerging challenges. Topic content awaits approval.

01 / Core topics

Airworthiness, from
fundamentals to frontiers.

Seven proposed topic areas. Each will grow into an explained, reviewed reference section written by society subject experts.

Fundamentals of airworthiness

What airworthiness means, how it is achieved, and how it is maintained across an aircraft’s life.

Design & test adequacy

How design assurance, analysis, and testing demonstrate that an aircraft meets its certification basis.

Certification processes & practices

The stages of a certification programme: basis, compliance demonstration, and approval.

Military & civil airworthiness

Where military and civil certification philosophies meet, and how they differ in practice.

Unmanned aircraft systems (UAS)

Certification and airworthiness considerations for unmanned and autonomous aircraft.

Continuing airworthiness & safety

Maintenance, fleet experience, and safety management across the service life of an aircraft.

Emerging certification challenges

New propulsion, autonomy, and software-intensive systems reshaping how certification is argued.

Topic explanations are illustrative and will be written by society subject experts after approval. The society does not grant airworthiness approvals or statutory certifications.

02 / Airworthiness in India

A dedicated view of
the Indian ecosystem.

A proposed section covering the evolution of military airworthiness in India, the defence aerospace ecosystem, key milestones, and emerging challenges.

  1. Evolution of military airworthinessA proposed historical overview of how military airworthiness evolved in India.
  2. The defence aerospace ecosystemRegulators, design houses, production agencies, and certification stakeholders.
  3. Key technical milestonesProgrammes and approvals that shaped Indian airworthiness practice.
  4. Emerging challengesUAS, indigenous design, and modern certification expectations.
