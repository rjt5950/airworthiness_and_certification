Membership

A proposed home for enrolment, participation, and member services. Categories, benefits, and fees below are samples for design review.

01 / Categories & eligibility

A place for every
airworthiness professional.

Three proposed membership categories cover the breadth of the airworthiness and certification community. Names and eligibility rules are illustrative.

Fellow

Senior airworthiness and certification professionals who can contribute long experience, review society output, and guide programmes.

Illustrative category

Member

Practitioners from industry, regulators, academia, and defence aerospace who wish to take part in events, knowledge exchange, and forums.

Illustrative category

Student & young professional

Students, researchers, and early-career engineers building airworthiness and certification expertise.

Illustrative category

Category names and eligibility are samples. The approved constitution and membership rules will define them.

02 / Benefits

What membership
is proposed to offer.

A proposed benefits set, subject to approval. Member-only services such as the portal and directory need backend services and consent handling.

Events & programmes

Preferred access to conferences, workshops, lectures, and webinars.

Knowledge Centre

Access to technical papers, standards references, case studies, and expert lectures.

Newsletter & announcements

Regular society updates and a subscription to the newsletter.

Professional network

Connection with regulators, industry, academia, and fellow professionals.
